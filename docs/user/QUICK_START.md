Astra is where our projects, tasks and deadlines live. This page covers what you
need on your first day.

## 1. Signing in

- Open **[ALY: the Astra address]** in a browser.
- Sign in with your email address and the temporary password you were given.
  **[ALY: confirm: the address and the password reach you by separate
  messages (the plan in `NEXT_STEPS.md` E2).]**
- Astra then asks you to **choose a new password**: type the temporary one, then
  your new one twice. It must have at least 8 characters and must differ from
  the temporary one. Until you change it, Astra shows nothing else.
- You stay signed in for 12 hours. To sign out, open the account menu (your
  initials, top right) and choose **Sign out**. **Sign out everywhere** also
  signs you out on your other devices.
- To change your password later: account menu, **Change my password**.
- **Forgot your password?** There is no reset link. Ask an App Owner, who sets a
  new temporary password for you; you choose your own at the next sign-in.

## 2. Finding your way

The bar on the left has:

- **Home**: what needs attention across the projects you can see.
- **My Work**: the tasks that are yours, as a list or a calendar.
- **Inbox**: notices for you, such as a password reset, or new dates when a
  linked schedule moves your task. New work, and the answer to work you
  submitted, are not announced there: check **My Work** and the task itself.
- **Projects**: every project you belong to. A project has the tabs
  **Overview**, **List**, **Board**, **Timeline** and **Activity**.

You see only the projects you have been added to (App Owners and the Chairman
see all of them).

## 3. Working on a task

- Click a task to open it. It shows its owner, start and due dates, criticality,
  progress, steps, attachments and its history.
- When your work is done, write a short note in **Submit work (note)** and
  press **Submit for acceptance**. The task's status is then locked while it is
  reviewed.
- An App Owner either accepts it (**Accept & complete**) or sends it back
  (**Request changes**, always with a reason). A project manager can ask the
  App Owner to do either. You are not notified of the answer: look at the task.
  If it comes back, make the changes and submit again.

## 4. The Board and "Move to…"

- A project's **Board** shows its tasks in columns: Draft, Ready, In progress,
  Blocked, Submitted, Accepted, Closed.
- Project managers (and App Owners) move cards by dragging, or with the
  **Move to…** control on each card, which also works from the keyboard and on
  a phone. Ordinary moves (between Draft, Ready and In progress) can be undone
  for 15 seconds. Some moves need an App Owner's approval; Astra says so and
  files the request.
- Everyone else sees the Board read-only.

## 5. "⚠ Needs a new assignee"

This mark means a task is assigned to someone who cannot carry it out (for
example the Chairman, or a project viewer on a main task). A project manager,
the Chairman or an App Owner should give it to someone else.

## 6. Who can do what

| Role | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| App Owner | Runs Astra: people, projects, approvals, files. |
| Chairman | Sees every project and its history; can assign work; is not given tasks. |
| Project manager | Edits the tasks of their project and moves cards; some changes go to an App Owner for approval. |
| Member | Sees their projects; works on and submits the tasks given to them. |
| Viewer | Sees the project; can be given subtasks only. |
