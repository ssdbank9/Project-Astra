## 5. Known defects and risks

### 5.1 F9HBSJ daily-summary scheduler spins on DST fall-back night

- **Where:** `seconds_until_next_digest` in `src/astra/daily_digest.py:18-28`.
- **Cause:** it builds the target time in the owner's zone and compares it to
  "now" by wall-clock reading. In the hour that repeats when clocks fall back,
  a delivery time whose first occurrence has already passed still looks ahead.
- **Effect:** the wait comes out as 0 seconds, so the scheduler loop queries
  SQLite nonstop instead of sleeping.
- **Repro:** `seconds_until_next_digest(datetime(2026,11,1,6,10,tzinfo=timezone.utc), "01:30", "America/New_York")` returns 0. There are 30 minutes of
  zero waits between 06:00 and 07:00 UTC.
- **Who is affected:** only an owner who picks a delivery time inside the
  repeated hour of a zone with DST. The default, 09:00 Asia/Karachi, is not.
- **Fix, when Aly says:**
  - compare the target and now as UTC instants, and roll to the next day when
    the target is not after now;
  - add tests for both DST changes, including a delivery time in the skipped
    spring hour;
  - then run the full suite and get an independent review.
